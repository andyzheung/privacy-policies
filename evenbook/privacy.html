账本数据

两清账无需注册应用账号。你添加的照护人员资料、出勤、工资与付款记录、开销、签字和票据附件保存在设备上，用于记账、核对工资和生成工资条。应用没有账本同步服务器，也没有广告或统计 SDK。

设备功能

启用提醒后，应用在获得你的允许后通过 iOS 安排本机通知。提醒不包含照护人员姓名或工资金额。可在应用内或 iOS 设置中关闭提醒。启用应用锁时，应用在本机钥匙串保存密码验证信息。Face ID 或 Touch ID 由 iOS 验证，应用不会读取生物识别数据。

备份与分享

你可主动导出备份或分享工资条等文件。接收应用或存储位置由你选择，文件副本由接收方管理。导出备份时，密码只加密账本数据文件。签字、票据等附件及备份清单仍可读取，请妥善保存备份。iOS 设备备份也可能包含本机应用数据，具体取决于你的设备与备份设置。了解 iCloud 备份内容。

App Store 订阅

Apple 处理 App Store 账户和付款资料。两清账使用 StoreKit 查询商品、发起购买、验证交易以确认 Pro 权益，并在你请求时恢复购买。Apple 的 App Store 隐私说明介绍了 Apple 如何处理这些数据。

保留与删除

可在应用内管理账本记录。删除应用可移除设备上的应用数据，但不会移除已导出的文件、接收方保存的副本或既有系统备份。这些副本需在各自的位置管理。

使用条款

Evenbook for iOS Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Developer and operator: Zhang Wenbo (individual developer)
Contact: andyzheung@aliyun.com

Ledger data

Evenbook does not require an app account. Caregiver details, attendance, pay and payment records, expenses, signatures, and receipt attachments you add are stored on your device. The app uses them to keep records, review pay, and create payslips. The app has no ledger sync server, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK.

Device features

If you enable reminders and grant notification permission, the app schedules them locally through iOS. Reminders omit caregiver names and pay amounts. You can turn reminders off in Evenbook or iOS Settings. If you enable App Lock, the app stores passcode verification data in the on-device Keychain. iOS handles Face ID or Touch ID verification. Evenbook does not read biometric data.

Backups and sharing

You can export backups or share files such as payslips. You choose the receiving app or storage location, and that destination manages its copy. A password on an exported backup encrypts the ledger data file only. Attachments, such as signatures and receipts, and the backup manifest remain readable. Store backups securely. An iOS device backup may also include local app data depending on your device and backup settings. What iCloud backs up.

App Store subscriptions

Apple handles App Store accounts and payment information. Evenbook uses StoreKit to look up products, initiate purchases, verify transactions to confirm Pro access, and restore purchases at your request. See Apple’s App Store privacy notice for how Apple handles that data.

Retention and deletion

You can manage ledger entries in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Exported files, copies held by recipients, and existing system backups are managed separately at their respective locations.
