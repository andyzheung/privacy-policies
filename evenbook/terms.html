记账与工资核对

两清账帮助你按录入的约定记录出勤、核对工资和整理凭证。应用不代为支付工资，也不认证工资计算或用工安排是否符合法律要求。请在确认或发放工资前与照护人员核对规则、记录和金额。

记录和文件

录入、导出或分享他人资料时，请确保你有权处理这些资料。换机、删除应用或恢复备份前，请先检查所需文件是否已保存。密码备份仅加密账本数据文件，签字、票据等附件及备份清单仍可读取。

Pro 订阅

可购买的方案、价格、计费周期和优惠以购买前 App Store 确认界面的显示为准。Apple 通过购买所用的 Apple 账户收款。自动续订订阅会持续续订，直到你取消。可在 iOS「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」中管理或取消订阅。删除应用不会取消订阅。

使用购买时的 Apple 账户，可在应用内选择「恢复购买」重新核验权益。Apple 的订阅取消说明。

Recordkeeping and pay review

Evenbook helps you record attendance, review pay, and organize documentation based on the terms you enter. The app does not transfer wages or certify that pay calculations or employment arrangements meet legal requirements. Review the rules, records, and amounts with the caregiver before confirming or paying wages.

Records and files

Only enter, export, or share another person’s information if you have permission to handle it. Check that you have saved the files you need before switching devices, deleting the app, or restoring a backup. A backup password encrypts the ledger data file only. Attachments, such as signatures and receipts, and the backup manifest remain readable.

Pro subscriptions

The App Store confirmation screen shows the available plans, prices, billing periods, and any offers before purchase. Apple bills the Apple Account used for the purchase. An auto-renewing subscription renews until you cancel. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings under your Apple Account and Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

To verify your access again, select Restore Purchases in the app using the Apple Account you purchased with. Apple’s subscription cancellation instructions.
