两清账 iOS 支持与常见问题

联系我们

遇到问题或有建议，请发邮件至 andyzheung@aliyun.com。请描述操作步骤、iOS 版本和应用版本；不要在邮件中发送照护人员的个人资料、工资记录或备份文件。

需要注册账号吗？

不需要应用账号。你输入的账本记录保存在设备上；本版本没有账本同步服务器。

如何保存和恢复记录？

可通过应用内「备份与恢复」手动导出和导入。换机或删除应用前，请先确认备份已保存，并在合适的时候检查能否恢复。备份密码仅加密账本数据文件，签字、票据等附件和备份清单仍可读取，请妥善保管。

如何恢复或取消 Pro 订阅？

使用购买时的 Apple 账户，在应用内选择「恢复购买」重新核验权益。管理或取消订阅请前往 iOS「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」。删除应用不会取消订阅。Apple 的订阅取消说明。

如何删除记录？

可以在应用内管理记录。删除应用会移除设备上的应用数据，但不会删除已导出的文件、接收方保存的副本或已有系统备份，请分别管理这些副本。

两清账会代发工资或提供税务建议吗？

不会。两清账用于记录和核对，请在确认或发放工资前核对约定、记录与金额；如需法律或税务意见，请咨询合适的专业人士。

Evenbook for iOS Support & FAQ

Contact

For help or feedback, email andyzheung@aliyun.com. Include the steps you took, your iOS version and app version. Please do not email caregiver personal details, pay records or backup files.

Do I need an account?

No app account is required. Ledger records you enter are stored on your device; this version has no ledger sync server.

How do I save or restore my records?

Use Backup & Restore in the app to export or import a backup manually. Before switching devices or deleting the app, check that your backup is saved and verify that it can be restored when appropriate. A backup password encrypts only the ledger data file; attachments such as signatures and receipts, and the backup manifest remain readable. Store backups securely.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

Use the Apple Account used for the purchase and choose Restore Purchases in the app. To manage or cancel a subscription, open iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple's subscription cancellation instructions.

How do I delete my data?

You can manage records in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data, but not exported files, copies held by recipients or existing system backups. Manage those copies separately.

Does Evenbook send wages or provide tax advice?

No. Evenbook is for recordkeeping and review. Check your terms, records and amounts before confirming or paying wages, and consult a qualified professional for legal or tax advice.
