EchoDiary Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Developer: Zhang Wenbo (张文博, individual developer)

EchoDiary (心语记, "the app") is an offline-first voice diary. This policy explains what the app processes, why, where it is stored, when any network access happens, and your rights. The Chinese version prevails in case of any inconsistency.

1. What we process

The app has no server, no account system and no third-party SDKs (no analytics, ads, push or crash reporting), and does not request the network permission itself. EchoDiary stores your diary on your device. We cannot access it. You can choose to export or back up your data as described in section 4.

2. Permissions

You can revoke permissions at any time in the system Settings. The app does not repeatedly ask after you deny.

If you choose Add in Calendar, EchoDiary prefills a generic title, your chosen time and a daily repeat in the system Calendar. EchoDiary does not read your calendar or pass any diary content. You can cancel or save the event, then edit or remove it in Calendar. The event follows the chosen calendar account and its sync settings and is not deleted if you uninstall EchoDiary.

3. When the network is used (by system services)

Transcription, mood analysis, keyword extraction and diary storage run on device and work offline. EchoDiary does not make network requests directly. System services may use the network in the following cases.

(1) Buying and restoring Pro through HUAWEI In-App Purchases (IAP Kit). Payment happens in the system cashier and may require your HUAWEI ID; the app never sees or stores payment details. After you consent to this policy, the app asks IAP Kit on each launch which products you own so Pro can be restored after reinstalling or switching phones; offline or signed out, this check silently fails. Huawei processes this data under its own privacy policy.

(2) Offline speech resources: if the offline Mandarin recognition or English voice is not yet installed, the system speech service may download it. No diary content is involved.

(3) System Calendar and backup: if you save a reminder in Calendar or use system backup, system services may sync data based on the account and settings you choose. See sections 2 and 4.

4. Storage and retention

Entries, moods, keywords and settings are stored in the app's private directory on your device. We have no server copy. Data is kept until you delete an entry or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's private data. TXT, Markdown or PDF files you export (Pro) are saved where you choose and remain after uninstalling. If you use the system Backup & Restore feature, the system may include this app's data in your backup.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or publicly disclose your personal information. EchoDiary uses HUAWEI In-App Purchases to process orders. See sections 2 through 4 for how system Calendar reminders and backups are handled. We may disclose information when required by law.

6. Your rights

You can view and correct entries and moods in the app, delete individual entries, and export TXT, Markdown or PDF copies (Pro). Uninstalling deletes the app's private data. You can withdraw consent in Settings > Withdraw privacy consent. The app then closes and asks again next time. You can also revoke permissions in system Settings. Because we hold no copy of your diary, these actions take effect on your device immediately.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults and not for children under 14. Minors should use it only with a guardian's consent. Guardians can delete entries, uninstall the app or contact us.

8. Updates

We will publish any update here with a new effective date. Material changes will be shown in the app the next time you open it, and your consent will be requested again.
