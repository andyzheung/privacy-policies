EchoDiary Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Developer: Zhang Wenbo (张文博, individual developer)

EchoDiary (心语记, "the app") is an offline-first voice diary. This policy explains what the app processes, why, where it is stored, when any network access happens, and your rights. The Chinese version prevails in case of any inconsistency.

1. What we process

The app has no server, no account system and no third-party SDKs (no analytics, ads, push or crash reporting), and does not request the network permission itself. Your diary never leaves your phone; we cannot access it.

3. When the network is used (by system services)

Transcription, mood analysis, keyword extraction, storage and reminders all run on device and work fully offline. Only these system services may use the network; the app itself makes no network requests:

(1) Buying and restoring Pro through HUAWEI In-App Purchases (IAP Kit). Payment happens in the system cashier and may require your HUAWEI ID; the app never sees or stores payment details. After you consent to this policy, the app asks IAP Kit on each launch which products you own so Pro can be restored after reinstalling or switching phones; offline or signed out, this check silently fails. Huawei processes this data under its own privacy policy.

(2) Offline speech resources: if the offline Mandarin recognition or English voice is not yet installed, the system speech service may download it. No diary content is involved.

4. Storage and retention

Entries, moods, keywords and settings are stored in the app's private directory on your phone. We have no server copy. Data is kept until you delete an entry or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes all local data. Files you export (Pro) are saved where you choose and are not removed on uninstall. If you use the system Backup & Restore feature, the system may include this app's data in your backup.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, transfer or publicly disclose your personal information. Apart from the purchase processing described in section 3, no data is provided to any third party, except where required by law.

6. Your rights

View and correct entries and moods in the app; delete single entries, or uninstall to delete everything; export entries (Pro); withdraw consent in Settings > Withdraw privacy consent (the app then closes and asks again next time); revoke permissions in system Settings. Because we hold no copy of your data, these actions take effect on your device immediately.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults and not for children under 14. Minors should use it only with a guardian's consent. Guardians can delete entries, uninstall the app or contact us.

8. Updates

We will publish any update here with a new effective date. Material changes will be shown in the app the next time you open it, and your consent will be requested again.
